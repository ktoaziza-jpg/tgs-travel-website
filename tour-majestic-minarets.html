Uzbekistan Majestic Minarets Tour

10 Days · 5 Legendary Cities · Starts in Tashkent, Ends in Samarkand

Where?

Step into the heart of Central Asia on an unforgettable 10-day journey through Uzbekistan's five most iconic Silk Road cities — from bustling Tashkent across the Kyzyl-Kum Desert to Khiva, Bukhara, Samarkand, and Shakhrisabz.

  • Old & Modern Tashkent + Oriental Bazaar
  • Khiva's Ichan-Kala & Bukhara's Kalyan Minaret
  • Samarkand's Registan & Timur's Birthplace Shakhrisabz

Who?

An easy-paced, fully guided cultural tour ideal for travelers (ages 19–80), solo explorers, couples, and history or photography lovers.

  • Easy physical level & comfortable pacing
  • Small groups (max 10 travelers)
  • Fully guided with private A/C transport & HST train

Highlights

Follow the skyline of minarets, turquoise domes, and living craft traditions from ancient Shash to the Green City of Shakhrisabz.

  • Crossing the Kyzyl-Kum Desert from Khiva to Bukhara
  • Konigil ancient silk paper mill & El Merosi theatre show
  • Sitorai Mokhi-Khosa Summer Palace & Naqshbandi Complex
Complete Uzbekistan Classic

Follow the Skyline of Ancient Minarets

This in-depth 10-day itinerary connects every jewel of Uzbekistan without rushing. Fly from Tashkent to Khiva's walled Ichan-Kala, cross the golden sands of the Kyzyl-Kum Desert to Bukhara's trading domes, and ride the high-speed train to Samarkand.

Beyond the grand monuments, experience Samarkand's traditional mulberry-paper craft in Konigil village, the historical costume spectacle at El Merosi Theatre, and a scenic excursion to Amir Timur's birthplace in Shakhrisabz.

3Stan Odyssey: Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan & Tajikistan

15 Days · 13 Destinations · Starts & Ends in Tashkent

Where?

Embark on an extraordinary 15-day, 14-night Silk Road odyssey across Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, and Tajikistan — covering 13 legendary stops from turquoise domes to desert flames and soaring mountain passes.

  • Uzbekistan: Tashkent, Khiva, Bukhara & Samarkand
  • Turkmenistan: Dashoguz, Darvaza Crater, Ashgabat & Merv
  • Tajikistan: Penjikent, Dushanbe, Istaravshan & Khujand

Who?

Designed for curious travelers (ages 14–60) with a moderate physical level who want a deep, fully guided cultural and adventure journey across three nations.

  • Small intimate groups (max 8 travelers)
  • Operated in English & Russian
  • Fully guided with seamless border assistance

Highlights

A rare combination of UNESCO World Heritage treasures, nomadic desert nights, and dramatic mountain roads in one seamless route.

  • Yurt night beside the burning Darvaza gas crater
  • UNESCO gems: Ichan-Kala, Old Nisa, Merv, Bukhara & Registan
  • High-altitude Istiklal & Shahristan mountain tunnels
Three Countries · One Epic Route

The Ultimate Central Asia Odyssey

Unravel the secrets of ancient Silk Road capitals — Tashkent, Khiva, Bukhara, and Samarkand — before crossing into the surreal Karakum Desert to sleep in yurts beside the glowing Darvaza crater and explore white-marble Ashgabat and ancient Merv.

Your journey culminates in mountainous Tajikistan, tracing Sogdian ruins in Penjikent, cultural landmarks in Dushanbe, ancient artisan quarters in Istaravshan, and Alexander the Great's historic Khujand before returning to Tashkent.
